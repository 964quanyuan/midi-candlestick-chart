Signal in motion

Music compositions translated into the language of market: notes become price ticks and candles, and dynamics become volatility. This project is makes the philosophical argument that composed modernist music and financial markets are both pseudo-chaotic systems where underlying signal erupts from a substrate of noise, both an amalgamation of human emotions and systematic principles, both defined by uncertainty in the future but sensibility in hindsight. This parallelism is visualized in real time by rendering one in the language of the other.
