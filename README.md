### Deliverable: [♭ar for ฿ar — MIDI-to-Market Visualizer](https://964quanyuan.github.io/midi-candlestick-chart/)

---

### System Architecture
**♭ar for ฿ar** translates classical piano etudes into animated financial candlestick charts in real time, synchronized directly to MIDI playback. The translation engine maps musical parameters to financial data through a structured pipeline:

* **Pitch-to-Price Mapping:** Sequential MIDI pitch deltas drive real-time price movements.
* **Interval Aggregation:** Notes triggered within uniform time slices are grouped into single OHLC (Open, High, Low, Close) candlesticks.
* **Entropy Injection:** Random pitch deltas are selectively discarded to introduce artificial market noise and friction.
* **Dynamic Modifiers:** Accented notes and velocity spikes apply directionally amplified modifiers, creating sudden volatility expansion and liquidity sweeps.

---

### Conceptual Premise
This project posits that modern classical music and financial markets share a deep structural identity. Both operate as pseudo-chaotic systems where clear signals emerge from a substrate of noise—complex amalgamations of systematic rules, human emotion, and systemic tension. 

Both domains are defined by absolute uncertainty in the moment, yet reveal undeniable logic and sensibility in hindsight. **♭ar for ฿ar** visualizes this parallelism in real time by translating the sonic canvas of avant-garde composition to the visual and structural language of high-finance trading.
